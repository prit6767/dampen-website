Our joint note

We build privacy-first focus technology with a simple conviction: doing better work should not require an expensive setup, a download, or giving up your data. After watching friends, classmates, and even ourselves lose hours to noisy rooms, shared spaces, and broken concentration, we built Deafen to put a better working environment into everyone's hands, entirely free to start. The experience runs on your own device rather than through an account wall, and every core feature is designed to feel instant. That simplicity is not a growth trick; it is the point.

Why we started

We are both 16 and still in high school, so learning in distracting environments was not some abstract productivity problem for us. It was normal life. Homework, studying, reading, and trying to lock in all felt harder than they needed to be. Deafen started from that frustration. We wanted to make focus better for everyone, especially people who do not have perfect setups, quiet rooms, or expensive tools.

How it works

What the actual company is building

Deafen is not trying to pretend the browser can become magical hardware. It is building the best no-signup focus experience people can open directly on the web.

Step 1 Deafen opens instantly and gives users a clear mode to choose from instead of making them configure a whole productivity stack.
Step 2 Each mode uses carefully shaped browser audio and a simple timer system to help reduce distraction, blur speech, or create a more stable work environment.
Step 3 Users come back because it feels useful in seconds, not because they finished onboarding or built a profile.

Core principles

  • Website-first
  • No signup barrier
  • Private by default
  • Fast enough for everyday use

What users actually get

  • Study focus modes
  • Speech blur experiences
  • Open-office shielding
  • Short reset sessions

The long-term company story is simple: help more people access a better focus environment without asking them to install something first or give up their privacy just to get started.
